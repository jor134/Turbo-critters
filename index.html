TURBOCRITTERS

Four circuits. Six critters. Grab a power-up and drift the corners.

How to Play

Steer with the thumbstick — hold your left thumb anywhere on the left side and slide it left or right. The further you push, the harder you turn. Your kart drives itself, so just point it at the track.
Drift around corners: hold 🌀 while turning, then let go for a boost. Blue sparks = small boost, orange = big boost.
Grab boxes ❓ on the track to get a power-up, then tap the big button to use it.
No walls — you can cut wide, but the further off the road you go the slower you get. Wander too far and you'll be lifted back on.
Hit the ramps 🟨 for big air. Longer flights land with a bigger boost.
Time Trial puts you alone on the circuit against a see-through ghost of the fastest recorded lap. The number under your lap time shows how far ahead (green) or behind (red) that ghost you are.
Beat a top-five lap and you enter your initials on that track's record board.
Grand Prix runs all four circuits back to back, solo or with friends (the host flips Cup: On in the room). Points are 10-8-6-4-3-2, and the most points at the end takes the cup.
Keyboard: ← → steer, Space drift, E item, S brake.
